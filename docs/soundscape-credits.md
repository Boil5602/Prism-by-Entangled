# Soundscapes — provenance

Twelve field recordings played by `tiles.prism/soundscape` during a music
service's ad breaks (docs/concept-scenes.md §6, dashboard-schema §26). Every
one is licensed **CC0 1.0** (Creative Commons Zero, public-domain dedication:
no attribution required, any use), chosen by the maintainer on 2026-09-08 and
fetched from Freesound's preview encodes (MP3). Each was trimmed to a loop by
copying whole MP3 frames (no re-encode) with the session's `trim_mp3.py`; the
page crossfades the seam. Credits are given anyway — the recordists earned them.
