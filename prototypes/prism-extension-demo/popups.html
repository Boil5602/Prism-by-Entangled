One-pan lemon and thyme chicken with new potatoes

Everything roasts together, the potatoes drink up the lemon, and there is one tin to wash. From Hesper Lindqvist's Tuesday column.

15 minto prepare
45 minin the oven
4people
Easyskill

You need

  • 8 chicken thighs, skin on
  • 750 g new potatoes, halved
  • 2 lemons, one sliced and one juiced
  • A small bunch of thyme
  • 6 cloves of garlic, left in their skins
  • 3 tablespoons of olive oil

Method

  1. Heat the oven to 200 C. Toss the potatoes in the tin with the oil, garlic and half the thyme.
  2. Sit the chicken on top, skin up. Tuck the lemon slices around it and pour over the juice.
  3. Roast for 45 minutes, turning the potatoes once, until the skin is crisp and deep gold.
Cook's note. No new potatoes? Cut floury ones into thumb-sized pieces and give them ten minutes in the oven before the chicken goes on.
