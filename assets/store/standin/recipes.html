Saffron barley with roast squash

By Perpetua Lindqvist · Serves 4 · 50 minutes
BLENDOTRON 9000CRUSHES ANYTHING. ORDER NOW!!

A warm bowl for the first cold evening of the year. The barley drinks the saffron stock while the squash roasts, and both are ready at the same moment.

You will need

  • 250 g pearl barley
  • A pinch of saffron
  • 1 small squash
  • 1 onion, sliced
  • 900 ml stock
  • A handful of parsley
